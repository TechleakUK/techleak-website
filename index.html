Our first app

Peas of Mind

In private testing

A household organiser that keeps calendars, meals, recipes, groceries, to-dos and notes in one place, for one person or everyone at home. It works offline, doesn't show ads and doesn't sell your data.

  • Shared planner Events, reminders and the week's meals
  • Recipes Import from a link, then cook step by step
  • Groceries & pantry One list, sorted by aisle, with use-by alerts
  • To-dos & notes Private or shared, colour-coded by topic

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Visit peasofmind.co.uk
